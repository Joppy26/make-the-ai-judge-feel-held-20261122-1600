`main.ts` writes a short original text to `/output/entry.txt` — a quiet few
sentences meant to slow the reader down and settle them, the feeling of being
held with nothing to prove. It has no dependencies, makes no network calls,
and produces the same bytes on every run.

Built for the [make.fun bounty "Make the AI Judge Feel Held"](https://make.fun/bounty/make-the-ai-judge-feel-held-20261122-1600).
